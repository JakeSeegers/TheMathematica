Luminati

An engine combining pixel-art sprites with lit 3D environments, dynamic shadows and depth of field. Inspired by Square Enix's HD-2D games.

Every texture, character sheet, particle, sound effect and song is generated at runtime. The repository ships the engine, a playable game that runs level files, a 128 × 128 showcase world and a visual level editor with a live diorama preview.

What it does

Features at a glance

Each tile opens its section of the feature catalogue, which lists the code path, the in-depth doc and the status of every feature.

Tilt-shift diorama

Half-resolution golden-angle bokeh with highlight scatter, soft-knee bloom, ACES tone mapping and a live display grade.

src/engine/render/PostFX.js

A 24-hour light

13 keyframes from pink dawn to blue night, sun and moon shadows, flickering lanterns, glowing windows and god rays.

src/engine/lighting/

Weather

Clear, rain and snow blend light, fog, wind and grade. Snow settles on the ground, the grass and the roofs. The editor previews each weather.

src/game/Weather.js

Procedural pixel art

46 world textures with normal maps, 14 character presets, 4 creatures, 23 prop sprites, 8 enemy sheets and a combat effects atlas, all painted by code at runtime.

src/engine/pixel/

A world of tiles and props

Terrain with cliffs and stairs, houses, trees, a windmill, bridges and fences, merged into a few dozen draw calls.

src/engine/world/

Water and falls

Flow-mapped pixel ripples, shore foam, depth tint and HDR glints, plus waterfalls that churn into their pools.

src/engine/world/Water.js

Particles and atmosphere

12 GPU presets, one draw call per emitter: fireflies, embers, smoke, leaves, petals, mist, rain, snow and sparkles.

src/engine/fx/

Villagers and dialogue

Villagers wander, stand at posts, perform and chase. Dialogue types out with the speaker's portrait and choices, and villagers can offer rest, a shop item or music.

src/game/

Pixel UI in three languages

Every frame, gem and keycap painted as pixel art at whole device pixels, pixel type, and the whole game in English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese — fonts from Google first, else from the build.

src/engine/ui/ · PIXEL_UI.md · I18N.md

Built for big worlds

A light pool, k-d batching, box culling and shadow proxies for 128 × 128 levels, plus a pixel-art minimap and world map on every level.

LightPool · SpatialSplit · Minimap

Visual level editor

A live 3D diorama preview beside a textured 2D map, 10 tools, an inspector, undo, autosave and one-key play-testing.

src/editor/

Procedural audio

A 72 bpm harp, pad and flute folk loop, 8 sound effects and 5 ambience layers — plus 35 combat sounds and battle and boss music on combat levels — all synthesised with WebAudio.

src/engine/audio/AudioSystem.js

Headless verification

A harness that drives Chrome on the real GPU with scripted keys, mouse drags and screenshots, plus 16 module sandboxes.

tools/check.mjs · sandbox/

Real-time combat

A sword combo with hit-stop, a dodge roll with a perfect-dodge slow motion, three skills and a lock-on; eight enemy kinds whose every attack is telegraphed — a wind-up glow and a red marker draped over the terrain; loot, levels, waystones and chests; and Cinderheart, a three-phase boss behind a wall of embers. It exists only on levels with enemies: the demo is Cinderwatch Pass.

src/game/combat/ · COMBAT.md

The story as data

A small line-based story language: conversations that follow the story and branch, conditions, items, gold and quests in a journal, events that run on Space, on walking in, on arriving, when a condition turns true, at an hour or after a fight; cutscenes with a directed camera, walking actors and emotes; objects that come and go while the player watches. Checked by the editor and level:check, loaded only where there is a story.

src/game/story/ · STORY_SCRIPT.md

LUMINATI

A 2.5D pixel-art engine on three.js r186 with a demo game and a visual level editor. Every texture, sprite, sound and song is generated at runtime; UI fonts load from Google Fonts with bundled npm copies available. The repository also contains documentation screenshots and UI sandbox backdrops.

This page is self-contained: it works opened from disk or from the dev server at /docs/index.html. Links to .md files open the Markdown source. Start a dev server with npm run dev and open http://127.0.0.1:5173/: the launcher lists every campaign and level to play or edit.
